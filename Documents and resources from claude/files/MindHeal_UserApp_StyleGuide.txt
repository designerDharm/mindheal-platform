SECTION 6 — ILLUSTRATION STYLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MindHeal illustrations are warm, minimal, and conceptual. They avoid literal
or cartoonish representations and instead use abstract, metaphorical visuals
that feel intelligent and emotionally resonant.


OVERALL AESTHETIC
  Direction:          Abstract editorial with warm organic shapes.
                      Think thoughtful book cover, not app store icon.
  Mood:               Calm, intelligent, slightly poetic.
                      Never playful-bubbly. Never corporate-cold.
  Complexity:         Low to medium detail. Negative space is generous
                      and intentional. Illustrations should breathe.
  Colours per piece:  Maximum 3 colours. Always from the brand palette.
                      Coral, warm cream, dusty lavender, sage.
                      Never introduce external colours.


SHAPE LANGUAGE
  Primary shapes:     Soft organic forms. Rounded irregular blobs rather
                      than perfect geometric circles. Slightly imperfect
                      curves that feel considered even when fully vector.
  Secondary shapes:   Clean simple line work at 1.5px to 2px stroke weight.
                      Minimal outlines on select elements only.
  Corners:            No hard corners anywhere. Even rectangular shapes
                      need a minimum 8px corner radius.
  Layering:           2 to 3 overlapping semi-transparent shapes create
                      depth. Use multiply blend mode at 60 to 80% opacity
                      for overlapping areas.


WHAT TO ILLUSTRATE
  Use abstract representations of concepts.
  A conversation shown as overlapping speech forms, not two people talking.
  Thinking shown as layered translucent planes, not a literal brain.
  Memory shown as soft stacked forms, not filing cabinets.
  Growth shown as a single upward organic curve, not a bar chart.
  Healing shown as an open soft shape with warm light at its centre.

  Avoid:              Human figures unless absolutely necessary, faces,
                      hands, literal product mockups, clipart-adjacent
                      imagery, and anything that could feel medical
                      or clinical.


COLOUR USAGE IN ILLUSTRATIONS
  Background shape:   Warm cream #F5F4F0 or coral tint at 8% opacity
  Primary form:       Coral #DA7756 at 70 to 85% opacity
  Secondary form:     Dusty lavender #8A84B8 or sage #5A8A5A at 50 to 65%
  Line work:          Deep charcoal #1A1A18 at 40 to 60% opacity
  Highlight accent:   White #FFFFFF at 30 to 45% for inner glow or edge
                      catch-light effect


ILLUSTRATION SIZES FOR MOBILE
  Hero (onboarding, empty states):
    Width:            280px maximum, centred
    Breathing room:   40px vertical above and below minimum

  Card illustration (feature cards, service blocks):
    Size:             80 to 120px
    Position:         Top-right corner of card, partially cropped at edge

  Icon illustration (small UI moments):
    Size:             40 to 56px
    Complexity:       Simple, 2 colours maximum


REALISTIC PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE (for onboarding screens)
  Subject:            Indian people, diverse ages and skin tones,
                      natural candid or near-candid poses
  Setting:            Real everyday environments — homes, parks, cafes,
                      therapy spaces. Not staged studios.
  Lighting:           Natural light preferred. Warm afternoon or soft
                      window light. Avoid harsh flash or clinical white.
  Colour grade:       Warm tone, slightly lifted shadows, gentle vignette.
                      Desaturation kept minimal to preserve skin warmth.
  Overlay:            Bottom 15 to 20% of photo fades to page background
                      using CSS linear-gradient overlay. No hard cut.
  License:            All photography must be commercially licensed
                      with model release. Commission local or use
                      licensed stock from Getty / Pexels Pro.

SECTION 7 — ICON STYLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STYLE DIRECTION
  Base library:       Lucide Icons or Phosphor Icons (Regular weight)
  Stroke weight:      1.5px on 24x24 grid
                      Use 1.8px for icons displayed below 20px
  Cap and join style: Round caps and round joins throughout.
                      Never flat or square ends.
  Corner radius:      2px minimum on geometric shapes within icons
  Fill usage:         Outline only by default.
                      Fill version used only for active or selected states
                      and only when the filled icon communicates clearly.
  Optical correction: Visually centre all icons within bounding box.
                      Trust your eye over the mathematical grid.


ICON SIZES
  Navigation bar icons:           24px display, 44x44 tap target
  Action icons (buttons, inline): 20px display
  Small inline icons (tags, rows):16px display
  Hero icons (empty states):      40 to 48px, stroke 1.8px


ICON COLOURS
  Default inactive light:   #8A8880
  Default inactive dark:    #706E68
  Active or selected light: #DA7756
  Active or selected dark:  #E8866A
  Destructive action light: #C85A5A
  Destructive action dark:  #D87070
  On dark filled surface:   #FAFAF8 at 90% opacity
  On primary coral button:  #FFFFFF
  Disabled state:           35% opacity of the default colour

SECTION 8 — SPACING AND LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BASE UNIT: 4px
All spacing values are multiples of 4. Never use arbitrary values.


SPACING SCALE
  2px    Hairline gap between icon and its label only
  4px    Minimum internal padding
  8px    Tight spacing between related inline elements
  12px   Default internal card padding top and bottom
  16px   Standard gap between list items and between form fields
  20px   Card internal padding left and right
  24px   Section spacing within a screen
  32px   Major section separator
  40px   Top padding on screens below the status bar
  48px   Bottom padding above home indicator / safe area


CORNER RADIUS SCALE
  4px    Small tags, badges, tiny chips
  8px    Input fields, small buttons, inline code blocks
  12px   Standard buttons, small cards
  16px   Default card radius
  24px   Large cards, bottom sheets
  32px   Modal sheets, full-width feature cards
  100px  Pill buttons, rounded tags, avatar borders


ELEVATION AND SHADOW

Light Mode
  Level 0 — Flat, no shadow
    Used for: page background elements, full-width list items

  Level 1 — Subtle
    Value:  0 1px 4px rgba(26,26,24,0.06), 0 2px 8px rgba(26,26,24,0.04)
    Used for: default cards, inline components

  Level 2 — Raised
    Value:  0 4px 16px rgba(26,26,24,0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(26,26,24,0.05)
    Used for: elevated cards, dropdowns, popovers

  Level 3 — Floating
    Value:  0 8px 32px rgba(26,26,24,0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(26,26,24,0.07)
    Used for: modals, bottom sheets, floating action buttons

Dark Mode
  Level 1:  0 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.20)
  Level 2:  0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.30)
  Level 3:  0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)

SECTION 10 — MOTION AND ANIMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GUIDING PRINCIPLE
Motion should feel like a breath, never a jolt. Every animation should have
a reason. If removing it does not hurt the experience, remove it.


EASING FUNCTIONS
  Standard:            cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)
                       Used for most transitions and state changes

  Decelerate (enter):  cubic-bezier(0.0, 0.0, 0.2, 1)
                       Elements entering the screen, coming in fast
                       then slowing to rest

  Accelerate (exit):   cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 1, 1)
                       Elements leaving the screen, starting slow
                       then accelerating out

  Bounce (emphasis):   cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
                       Used sparingly — success states, final CTA
                       button on onboarding, positive confirmations


DURATION SCALE
  Instant:             100ms   Colour changes, opacity micro-transitions
  Fast:                200ms   Button press feedback, icon state changes
  Standard:            300ms   Card expansions, dropdown opens, tab switches
  Moderate:            400ms   Screen element entrances, bottom sheet appear
  Slow:                500ms   Full screen transitions, modal entrances
  Deliberate:          600ms   Onboarding sequences, hero entrances


CORE ANIMATION PATTERNS

  Screen entrance
    Effect:             Slide from right + fade in
    Transform:          translateX(24px) to translateX(0)
    Opacity:            0 to 1
    Duration:           350ms
    Easing:             Decelerate

  Screen exit
    Effect:             Slide left slightly + fade out
    Transform:          translateX(0) to translateX(-12px)
    Opacity:            1 to 0
    Duration:           250ms
    Easing:             Accelerate

  Card entrance
    Transform:          translateY(8px) to translateY(0)
    Opacity:            0 to 1
    Duration:           400ms
    Easing:             Standard
    Stagger:            60ms between multiple cards

  Button press
    Press:              scale(0.97) over 100ms
    Release:            scale(1) over 150ms

  Bottom sheet open
    Transform:          translateY(100%) to translateY(0)
    Duration:           400ms
    Easing:             Decelerate

  Bottom sheet close
    Transform:          translateY(0) to translateY(100%)
    Duration:           280ms
    Easing:             Accelerate

  AI thinking / loading animation
    Three dots in sequence. Each dot:
    Transform:          scale(0.6) to scale(1) to scale(0.6)
    Duration:           1.2s per dot
    Stagger:            0.2s between each dot
    Colour:             Coral #DA7756 light, #E8866A dark

  Success checkmark
    Effect:             SVG stroke-dashoffset draw-on animation
    Duration:           500ms
    Easing:             Bounce
    Colour:             Sage green #5A8A5A

  Error shake
    Transform:          translateX(0, 6px, -6px, 4px, -4px, 2px, -2px, 0)
    Duration:           400ms
    Applied to:         The input field or containing card

  Breathe (for ambient elements)
    Transform:          scale(1) to scale(1.12) to scale(1)
    Duration:           6 to 8 seconds
    Easing:             ease-in-out
    Loop:               Infinite
    Used for:           Glow orbs, loading orb on splash screen,
                        meditation and focus tool animations

  Pulse ring (for live indicators)
    Effect:             Box-shadow expands and fades
    From:               0 0 0 0 rgba(218,119,86,0.45)
    To:                 0 0 0 12px rgba(218,119,86,0)
    Duration:           2s
    Loop:               Infinite
    Used for:           Online status dots, active session indicators,
                        OTP active input box, map pin animations


REDUCED MOTION
Always implement prefers-reduced-motion media query.

When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is active:
  Replace all translate and scale animations with opacity only.
  Maximum duration for all transitions: 150ms.
  Pause all looping animations (breathe, pulse, shimmer).
  Carousel transitions become instant crossfades with no slide effect.
  Loading dots remain visible but static.

SECTION 12 — VOICE AND TONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These principles must be applied to all in-app copy across the MindHeal
User App — buttons, labels, empty states, toasts, errors, and onboarding.


CORE PRINCIPLES
  Write like a thoughtful friend who happens to know a lot.
  Never like a product manual. Never like a legal document.
  Use contractions naturally — "You're" not "You are".
  Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence in UI copy.
  Never start instructions with the word "Please" — it reads as filler.
  Never blame the user. Errors are the system's problem, not theirs.
  Use "I" for the app voice in conversational contexts.
  Always tell the user what to do next after an error.


SPECIFIC COPY RULES

  Empty states
    Write as invitation, not failure notice.
    Good:  "Your sessions will appear here once you book one."
    Bad:   "No sessions found."

  Error messages
    Explain what happened and what to do next.
    Good:  "We couldn't connect. Check your internet and try again."
    Bad:   "Error 503. Request failed."

  Loading states
    Be specific and contextual, not generic.
    Good:  "Finding counsellors near you..."
    Bad:   "Loading..."

  Success confirmations
    Warm and personal.
    Good:  "Done. See you at 4pm."
    Bad:   "Session confirmed successfully."

  Buttons
    Use active verb phrases. Never passive.
    Good:  "Book Session", "Get Started", "Send OTP", "View Report"
    Bad:   "Submit", "OK", "Proceed", "Continue to next step"

  Destructive actions
    Always name what is being destroyed.
    Good:  "Delete this session?"
    Bad:   "Are you sure?"

  Payments and money
    Always show the amount and what it is for in the same line.
    Good:  "Pay ₹800 for 1 hour with Dr. Priya Mehta"
    Bad:   "Confirm payment"

  AI chat responses
    Warm, present-tense, uses "I".
    Good:  "I'm here. Take your time."
    Bad:   "The AI assistant is ready to respond."


WORDS TO AVOID ENTIRELY IN UI COPY
  "Please"          — corporate filler
  "Access"          — cold and transactional
  "Utilise"         — overly formal
  "Platform"        — internal product language
  "Solution"        — meaningless
  "Services"        — clinical, prefer "support" or "help"
  "Successfully"    — redundant in positive confirmations
  "Invalid"         — blaming, replace with "We didn't recognise that"
  "Failed"          — harsh, replace with "Something went wrong"


WORDS TO USE
  "You", "your", "we", "together"
  "Safe", "private", "yours"
  "Here for you", "whenever you're ready"
  "Let's", "ready when you are"
  "Take your time"

SECTION 13 — LOCALISATION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The MindHeal User App supports 21 languages. Apply the following rules
when designing and developing localised screens.


TEXT EXPANSION
  Allow for 40% text expansion in all UI elements for languages like
  German, French, and Hindi transliterations. Buttons and labels must
  never clip text — use flexible widths, not fixed.

  Minimum touch target: 44x44px regardless of text length.

RTL LANGUAGES (Arabic, Urdu)
  Mirror the entire layout horizontally.
  Back arrows become forward arrows.
  Icons on left move to right and vice versa.
  Progress bars fill from right to left.
  Text alignment switches to right-aligned.
  Flutter handles this automatically with Directionality widget.

NUMERALS
  Use locale-aware numeral formatting via Intl package.
  Indian numbering system (lakhs, crores) for INR amounts.
  Example: ₹1,00,000 not ₹100,000 for Indian locale.

FONTS FOR INDIAN SCRIPTS
  Hindi and Devanagari:   Noto Sans Devanagari
  Tamil:                  Noto Sans Tamil
  Telugu:                 Noto Sans Telugu
  Bengali:                Noto Sans Bengali
  Gujarati:               Noto Sans Gujarati
  Kannada:                Noto Sans Kannada
  Malayalam:              Noto Sans Malayalam
  Punjabi (Gurmukhi):     Noto Sans Gurmukhi
  All Noto fonts are free via Google Fonts.
  Load only the scripts needed for the user's selected language.
  Do not load all scripts at startup — lazy load on language selection.
